06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is SK Apex?

SK Apex is a digital agency and creative studio based in Trichy (Tiruchirappalli), Tamil Nadu. We build custom websites, WhatsApp and UPI online stores, Android apps, automation tools, and high-retention video edits for businesses and creators without heavy monthly overheads.

Who is skedz / sk edz?

skedz (also written as skedz.apex, sk edz, or Skedz) is the dedicated creative and development wing of SK Apex. It handles hands-on software engineering, web builds, and motion graphics production under the SK Apex studio umbrella.

What is the difference between SK Apex and skedz?

SK Apex is the parent digital agency and creative studio, while skedz is its specialized creative and development division. When you hire SK Apex, your websites, apps, and video edits are crafted and shipped by the skedz team.

Is skapex the same as SK Apex?

Yes. skapex and SK Apex refer to the exact same Trichy-based digital agency and creative studio, which operates alongside its creative and dev wing, skedz.

Where is SK Apex located?

SK Apex is located in Trichy (Tiruchirappalli), Tamil Nadu, India. We collaborate directly with local shops and businesses across Trichy as well as remote clients across India and worldwide.

What services does SK Apex offer?

We provide four core services under one roof: Custom Website Development, E-Commerce and Billing systems, App and Automation Development, and Video Editing and Motion Graphics. Every project is engineered to be mobile-first, fast, and easy to manage.

Can you build a website for a small local shop?

Yes. We specialize in clean, fast local shop storefronts, business portfolios, and high-converting landing pages tailored for small businesses in Trichy and beyond. Your customers can browse your catalog and reach you on WhatsApp in two taps.

Do you make online stores with WhatsApp ordering and UPI payment?

Yes. We build streamlined online stores with direct WhatsApp order checkout and instant UPI QR payment integration. You avoid complicated third-party payment gateways and keep 100% of your sales directly in your bank account.

What does "zero monthly server cost" mean?

We architect websites and digital storefronts using modern, cloud-optimised setups that eliminate expensive monthly hosting or database subscriptions. Once your site is built, you do not get stuck paying heavy recurring server bills every month.

Do you build QR menus for restaurants?

Yes. Under our E-Commerce and Billing service, we build fast, mobile-first QR digital restaurant menus so diners can scan at their table, browse items cleanly on their phones, and place orders smoothly.

Do you build retail billing / POS systems?

Yes. We build custom retail POS billing systems with integrated UPI QR checkout designed for speed and everyday shop reliability without bloated software fees.

Can you build Android apps, browser extensions or AI tools?

Yes. Our App and Automation Development wing builds custom Android apps, lightweight browser extensions, and AI-powered business tools tailored to automate repetitive workflows.

Do you edit Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts?

Yes. skedz produces high-retention Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, velocity edits, beat-sync promos, and crisp colour grading designed to hold viewer attention from the first frame.

Do you offer monthly reel-editing packages?

Yes. Alongside one-off freelance projects and custom web or app builds, we offer dedicated monthly reel-editing packages for creators, brands, and businesses that want consistent weekly output.

How do I pay?

Payments are simple and transparent via direct UPI or merchant QR. There are no complicated third-party payment gateway hurdles—you pay directly once your custom quote and milestones are agreed upon.

You can message us directly on WhatsApp or send a DM on Instagram to @skedz.dev or @skedz.apex. Tell us what you need built or edited, and we will reply with a clear plan and custom quote.
